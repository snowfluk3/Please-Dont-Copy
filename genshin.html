Gameplay & Mechanics

This section is all about some simple gameplay within Genshin.
Hover over one of the three buttons to get additional information.

Mechanics

Basic Controls and Gameplay - Keqing Example

Genshin Impact uses a 4-Character
party system that allows for
quick-swapping between
characters.

Characters will consume
stamina doing actions like climbing,
sprinting, and gliding.

Basic Attack

Attack and deal physical damage. Holding the attack button unleashes a stronger, charged attack that costs stamina. Use in the air to initiate a plunge attack.

Elemental Skill

Launch a lightning stiletto that deals damage. Use charged attack to detonate or use SKILL to teleport/damage with ELECTRO.

Elemental Burst

Engulfed in rapid lightning, quickly use rapid strikes against all enemies within an area and deal AoE ELECTRO damage.

Geo Reactions

Crystallize Reaction

When PYRO/HYDRO/CRYO/ELECTRO comes into contact with GEO, CRYSTALLIZE will occur. When this happens, shards of the corresponding element will be formed. Picking up will form shields that also have increased effectiveness against each element's respective element.

Anemo Reactions

Swirl Reaction

When PYRO/HYDRO/CRYO/ELECTRO comes into contact with ANEMO, SWIRL will occur. When this happens, SWIRL occurs AoE elemental damage based on the element swirled. It is great at controlling crowds, and dealing high amounts of specified AoE elemental damage.

Pyro Reactions (Has Crystallize)

Vaporize Reaction

When PYRO comes into contact with HYDRO, deal increased PYRO damage. Multiplies PYRO damage by 1.5 plus extra Elemental Mastery damage.

Melt Reaction

When PYRO comes into contact with CRYO, deal increased PYRO damage. Multiplies PYRO damage by 2 plus extra Elemental Mastery damage.

Overloaded Reaction

When PYRO comes into contact with ELECTRO, cause an explosion of AoE PYRO damage. Explodes in a 5m radius, causes strong knockback, and gives 2.75 times damage multipliers alongside EM.

Swirl Reaction

When PYRO comes into contact with ANEMO, inflict SWIRL. Deal AoE PYRO damage when mixed with ANEMO, and deal more damage based on Elemental Mastery.

Burning Reaction

When PYRO comes into contact with DENDRO, inflict BURNING. Causes opponents afflicted to take ticks of PYRO damage over time. Applies PYRO to enemies a in 1m radius in 2 second intervals.

Burgeon Reaction

BURGEON is triggered when PYRO comes into contact with Dendro Cores. Dendro Cores are formed when HYDRO and DENDRO interact. Upon PYRO detonating Dendro Cores, an explosion that deals AoE DENDRO damage occurs.

Hydro Reactions (Has Crystallize)

Vaporize Reaction

When HYDRO comes into contact with PYRO, deal increased HYDRO damage. Multiplies HYDRO damage by 2 plus extra Elemental Mastery damage.

Frozen Reaction

When HYDRO comes into contact with CRYO, inflict FROZEN. FROZEN enemies cannot move, and will only thaw after a short while or after being hit with PYRO. SHATTER can be inflicted using a greatsword, dealing increased PHYSICAL damage.

Electro-Charged Reaction

When HYDRO comes into contact with ELECTRO, ELECTRO-CHARGED occurs. ELECTRO-CHARGED causes ELECTRO damage over time to enemies afflicted or enemies nearby inflicted with HYDRO. Deals 2 times multipliers.

Swirl Reaction

When HYDRO comes into contact with ANEMO, SWIRL occurs. Deal AoE HYDRO damage when mixed with ANEMO, and deal more damage based on Elemental Mastery.

Bloom Reaction

When HYDRO comes into contact with DENDRO, BLOOM occurs. Upon BLOOM, a Dendro Core will be created. After 6 seconds, the Core will explode and deal AoE DENDRO damage. Cores can be interacted with PYRO and ELECTRO.

Burgeon Reaction

BURGEON is triggered when PYRO comes into contact with Dendro Cores. Dendro Cores are formed when HYDRO and DENDRO interact. Upon PYRO detonating Dendro Cores, an explosion that deals AoE DENDRO damage occurs.

Hyperbloom Reaction

HYPERBLOOM is triggered when ELECTRO comes into contact with Dendro Cores. Dendro Cores are formed when HYDRO and DENDRO interact. Upon ELECTRO detonating Dendro Cores, a Sprawling Shot will be formed and will home in on nearby enemies. Deals high DENDRO damage in a 1m radius.

Cryo Reactions

Melt Reaction

When CRYO comes into contact with PYRO, deal increased CRYO damage. Multiplies CRYO damage by 1.5 plus extra Elemental Mastery damage.

Frozen Reaction

When CRYO comes into contact with HYDRO, inflict FROZEN. FROZEN enemies cannot move, and will only thaw after a short while or after being hit with PYRO. SHATTER can be inflicted using a greatsword, dealing increased PHYSICAL damage.

Superconduct Reaction

When CRYO comes into contact with ELECTRO, inflict SUPERCONDUCT. SUPERCONDUCT deals AoE CRYO damage in a 5m radius and lowers PHYSICAL resistance by 40% for 12 seconds.

Swirl Reaction

When CRYO comes into contact with ANEMO, SWIRL occurs. Deal AoE CRYO damage when mixed with ANEMO, and deal more damage based on Elemental Mastery.

Crystallize Reaction

When CRYO comes into contact with GEO, CRYSTALLIZE occurs. CRYSTALLIZE triggered using CRYO will create a shield of CRYO that nullifies damage and staggering alongside a 250% increased resistance against CRYO attacks.

Electro Reactions (Has Crystallize)

Overloaded Reaction

When ELECTRO comes into contact with PYRO, cause an explosion of AoE PYRO damage. Explodes in a 5m radius, causes strong knockback, and gives 2.75 times damage multipliers alongside EM.

Aggravate Reaction

When ELECTRO comes into contact with CATALYZE, AGGRAVATE occurs. CATALYZE is inflicted when DENDRO and ELECTRO interact. Upon AGGRAVATE, deal 1.15 times more ELECTRO damage plus EM.

Spread Reaction

When DENDRO comes into contact with CATALYZE, SPREAD occurs. CATALYZE is inflicted when DENDRO and ELECTRO interact. Upon SPREAD, deal 1.25 times more DENDRO damage plus EM.

Hyperbloom Reaction

HYPERBLOOM is triggered when ELECTRO comes into contact with Dendro Cores. Dendro Cores are formed when HYDRO and DENDRO interact. Upon ELECTRO detonating Dendro Cores, a Sprawling Shot will be formed and will home in on nearby enemies. Deals high DENDRO damage in a 1m radius.

Enemy Types

Basic Enemies

Basic enemies are weak enemies you can find all around the overworld. Most tend to be in groups.

Elite Enemies

Elite enemies are powerful foes that each have different abilities. Overcome them for a challenge.

Boss Enemies

Bosses are the strongest of all enemies. With many tricks hidden up their sleeves, either through brute force, or a leveled playing field, bosses are very formidable.

Characters




Boss of Spina Di Rosula

Navia

Navia

Helm of the Radiant Rose

Blunt Refusal - Basic Attack

Ceremonial Crystalshot - Elemental Skill

As the Sunlit Sky's Singing Salute -
Elemental Burst

Navia is a GEO character that wields a CLAYMORE specializing in close range GEO burst damage. Upon picking up shards from the CRYSTALLIZE reaction, collect stacks that increase SKILL damage up to a cap of 6. Using BURST has GEO cannonballs rain down from the sky, each shot increasing Navia's stacks.

She is the optimistic president of the Spina di Rosula, a mantle previously held by her late father Callas. Her enthusiastic and tenacious spirit allow her to push on with helping the people of Fontaine in spite of the various losses in her life.

Yuheng of Liyue

Keqing

Keqing

Driving Thunder

Yunlai Swordsmanship - Basic Attack

Stellar Restoration - Elemental Skill

Starward Sword - Elemental Burst

Keqing is an agile SWORD-wielding character specializing in quick bursts of ELECTRO damage. Using SKILL places a lightning stiletto that can be used a burst attack or transporation method. BURST unleashes a flurry of ELECTRO attacks that increases Keqing's Crit. rate.

As the Yuheng of the Liyue Qixing, she is someone who seeks her own answers instead of idly letting chaos run amok in Liyue. She chooses her own path with her own power and ability, instead of letting the gods determine her fate.

Wanderer

Wanderer

Eons Adrift

Yuuban Meigen - Basic Attack

Hanega: Song of the Wind - Elemental Skill

Kyougen: Five Ceremonial Plays -
Elemental Burst

Wanderer is a CATALYST user that unleashes sharp strikes of ANEMO damage. SKILL gives Wanderer the ability to fly for a brief time. Hitting enemies afflicted with PYRO/CRYO/HYDRO/ELECTRO provides buffs depending on the element. BURST launches strong gale of ANEMO damage.

The Wanderer came into existence in place of his previous incarnation after the latter expunged his previous appellations and their respective histories from Irminsul. Harboring his former self's memories after willingly regaining them, "Wanderer" is now the only title he goes by, for he has no home, kin, or destination.

Furina

Endless Solo of Solitude

Soloist's Solicitation - Basic Attack

Salon Solitaire - Elemental Skill

Let the People Rejoice - Elemental Burst

Furina utilizes a SWORD with elegance, and uses HYDRO to overwhelm foes. In Ousia, Furina summons three companions that deal high HYDRO damage, but consume party HP. Everytime a party member is healed, gain a stack that increases damage and healing.

Introduced as the flamboyant and overconfident Hydro Archon, Furina's theatrics are eventually revealed to be a public persona, which she later discards in favor of living a relatively humbler life as an actress and artistic consultant.

Elemental Burst

Alhaitham is skeptic SWORD-wielder that holds a DENDRO vision. SKILL causes Alhaitham to rush forward, and holding SKILL allows you to aim that SKILL. Causes Alhaitham's attacks to deal DENDRO damage. BURST summons a binding field that deals multiple instances of DENDRO damage in an area.

Alhaitham is a member of the Haravatat of the Sumeru Akademiya and the Akademiya's Scribe, responsible for documenting their findings and drafting ordinances. Despite his brilliance, people within the Akademiya are perplexed by Alhaitham due to his unwillingness to aspire for higher office in spite of his egocentricity, and many have been at odds with him for his uncompromising view on rationality and the truth.

Hu Tao

Fragrance in Thaw

Secret Spear of Wangsheng - Basic Attack

Guide to Afterlife - Elemental Skill

Spirit Soother - Elemental Burst

Hu Tao is a peculiar girl that fights with a polearm, and seems to have a penchant for death utilizing PYRO. SKILL sacrifices a portion of Hu Tao's health to infuse her polearm with PYRO, allowing Hu Tao to deal very high PYRO damage. BURST causes Hu Tao to deal a high amount of PYRO damage close to her, and heals HP for each enemy hit.

Hu Tao's antics and eccentricity belies her role as the 77th Director of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor and her talent as a poet. Nevertheless, she treats the parlor's operations with utmost importance, and holds funeral ceremonies with the highest dignity and solemnity.
